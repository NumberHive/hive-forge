Forge the Third

How to play
  1. Every triplet is three cards worth the same amount. Two are given. You forge the third.
  2. Use the dials to build a number, or cut and shade a diagram. Press Forge it to check.
  3. First try is 3 points, second try 2, third try 1. After three misses you can reveal it for 0.
  4. Simplify bonus: shrink your new card to its simplest form by dividing the top and bottom by the same number. Worth 2, plus 1 for a clean finish.
  5. Level 2 locks the bottom number (or the number of parts) so you must build the unsimplified version, like 6/12, then simplify it back.
